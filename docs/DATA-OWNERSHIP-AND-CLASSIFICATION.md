# MylesNet data ownership and classification

**Task:** MN-PLAN-006 · **Plan phase:** Phase 0 — close planning and turn decisions into build contracts<br>
**Status:** Planning contract; no product data store or workflow is implemented.<br>
**Authority:** `AGENTS.md`, `READINESS-DECISIONS.md`, `DECISION-REGISTER.md`, `TECHNICAL-SPECIFICATION.md` §§8, 14.2–14.8, and the Phase 0 ADRs.

## Purpose and limits

This inventory assigns an authoritative source, tenant/branch scope, working sensitivity class, retention category, and failure/recovery obligation to critical records and events. It is an application planning contract, not a legal classification scheme or approved retention schedule. The exact periods, country rules, controller/processor roles, legal holds, storage provider, RPO/RTO, and named operational owners remain open. Open ownership is recorded as an unassigned accountable role rather than guessed.

The terms **authoritative source** and **projection** are distinct. A projection may serve a fast UI or runtime path, but cannot silently overwrite facts owned by another system. Reconciliation records mismatches and their resolution.

## Working sensitivity labels

These labels are proposed handling levels for engineering contracts. MN-PLAN-008 and privacy/legal owners must ratify them before production processing.

| Label | Working meaning | Handling expectation |
|---|---|---|
| `PUBLIC` | Explicitly approved for public release | No tenant-private or unpublished information; review content before publication. |
| `INTERNAL` | Non-public operational or configuration information with limited direct personal impact | Authenticated workforce access; do not expose through public caches or anonymous endpoints. |
| `CONFIDENTIAL` | Tenant business data, personal data, financial records, customer/service details, or information whose disclosure harms a tenant or person | Tenant/branch and purpose scoped; least-privilege access, redacted telemetry, retention and export controls. |
| `RESTRICTED` | Credentials, secrets, payment/authentication tokens, recovery evidence, high-risk security/access records, or data enabling direct privileged access | Secret store or tightly controlled security service; never return secret values to browser/logs; step-up and explicit audit for permitted operations. |

Classification applies to copies, derived records, exports, caches, traces, backups, and support views. An aggregate is not automatically public: small cohorts or stable identifiers can re-identify a person or tenant.

## Scope invariants

- Every tenant-owned record, event, file/object, export, search document, cache key, and job payload carries a server-derived `tenantId`. Tenant ownership does not change except through a separately audited migration.
- A branch-scoped resource has a verified branch assignment; a tenant-wide resource is explicitly declared tenant-wide. `null` must not mean both unassigned and tenant-wide. Exact resource/branch semantics remain open under MN-DEC-016; see the [decision packet](BRANCH-RESOURCE-SCOPE-DECISION-PACKET.md).
- Identity, role, tenant lifecycle, permission, resource assignment, and branch scope are rechecked at every operation and live delivery. Client-supplied tenant/branch IDs are never authorization.
- Keep WorkOS staff identity, subscriber identity, and RADIUS credentials separate by default. Application membership and permission records remain distinct from WorkOS organization membership.
- Keep MylesNet platform subscription ledgers and each ISP's subscriber-collection ledger separate. Settled financial facts and audit events are append-only; corrections use linked reversals/adjustments.
- Secrets are stored as references to managed secret storage, never plaintext application fields. Raw card data is not stored; provider-hosted checkout/tokenization is preferred.
- Host, IP, and tenant-supplied branding are routing/presentation input only. They never prove an actor's identity or permission.

## Retention and legal-hold register

No exact retention period is approved by this document. The project uses country- and category-specific schedules with legal holds overriding deletion. Local counsel must validate the periods and transfers before production processing in Kenya, Uganda, or Tanzania; use the [country legal and data-protection evidence plan](COUNTRY-LEGAL-AND-DATA-PROTECTION-EVIDENCE-PLAN.md) to record country-specific advice and approvals.

| Category key | Applies to | Current decision | Accountable owner needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RET-IAM` | Identity, membership, authentication, recovery | Revoke access immediately; retain only necessary audit/history. Period open. | Identity/security owner and country counsel |
| `RET-SUBSCRIBER` | Subscriber profile, service and contact data | Purpose-limited; exact country periods open. | Subscriber product/data owner and country counsel |
| `RET-FINANCIAL` | Tenant and platform ledgers, invoices, refunds, taxes, reconciliation | Immutable facts; statutory retention periods open by country/legal entity. | Finance/tax owner and country counsel |
| `RET-RADIUS` | NAS, credentials references, authentication/accounting, sessions | Category schedule required; exact period and purge/projection behavior open. | Network operations owner and country counsel |
| `RET-NETWORK` | Router/NAS registration, tenant network configuration, device health and router-job metadata | Minimize after decommission; preserve only operational and incident evidence required by policy. Exact periods open. | Network operations owner and country counsel |
| `RET-SECRET` | Secret references and credential rotation/revocation metadata; excludes secret values | Keep secret values only in the managed secret store. Retain lifecycle metadata only as needed for security/accountability; exact period open. | Security/secret-management owner and country counsel |
| `RET-CONSENT` / `RET-COMMUNICATION` | Consent, suppression, notices, message delivery | Preserve evidence needed for purpose/opt-out; exact periods open. | Communications/privacy owner and country counsel |
| `RET-AUDIT` | General business-operation audit records not covered by a more specific category | Append-only while retained; preserve actor, action, time, reason and safe result. Exact period open. | Product/security owner and country counsel |
| `RET-SECURITY` | Provider callback verification, replay/abuse evidence and integration-security records | Minimize raw payloads; preserve verification outcome, source reference and incident evidence. Exact period open. | Security/integration owner and country counsel |
| `RET-SECURITY-AUDIT` | Role changes, sensitive actions, support grants, security events | Append-only while retained; exact period and legal holds open. | Security/privacy owner and country counsel |
| `RET-TENANT` / `RET-OPERATIONAL` | Tenant settings, domains, jobs, equipment, configuration | Apply approved purpose/lifecycle rules; exact periods open. | Product/data owner and country counsel |
| `RET-EXPORT` | Generated exports and temporary files | Expiring/revocable access required; exact maximum retention and backup expiry open. | Data/storage owner and privacy counsel |
| `RET-TELEMETRY` | Logs, traces, metrics, incident artifacts | Minimize and redact; retention/cardinality periods open. | Service security/operations owners |
| `RET-REFERENCE` | Private screenshot and design/reference captures | Keep local-only and minimized; exact local retention/backup policy open. | Project/reference owner |

For every deletion or anonymization job, record the data categories and sources visited, legal-hold decision, result, retries, and proof. Include primary stores, files, search/indexes, caches, analytics, logs, and backup expiry. Service suspension/offboarding is separate from erasure. Exact schedules are a MN-PLAN-007/legal gate; the data owner and legal owner must be named by MN-PLAN-008.

## Failure, recovery, and evidence obligations

| Boundary | Required behavior | Evidence still required | Owner state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authorization/context resolution | Deny on unknown tenant, stale membership, insufficient role, branch mismatch, suspended state, or ambiguous resource scope; stop unauthorized live updates. | Cross-tenant/branch negative cases, cache-key review, stale-session and revocation acceptance. | Application security owner unassigned. |
| Provider callback/payment | Verify and durably accept raw event before acknowledgement; validate mapping, intent, amount and currency before financial effect; quarantine mismatch; reconcile idempotently. | Provider-specific signatures, replay windows, settlement behavior, reconciliation and one-effect/one-activation evidence. | Finance/integration owner unassigned. |
| RADIUS/AAA | Keep Convex out of request path; durable PostgreSQL/outbox; reject new authentication on approved outage; preserve current sessions where possible. | PostgreSQL/RADIUS failover, queue-capacity, recovery-drain, NAS mismatch and stale policy evidence. | Network owner unassigned. |
| WorkOS/application state | Do not grant app access from provider membership alone; recheck internal current access. | MFA spike, organization mapping and recovery tests, delayed/duplicate event handling. | Identity owner unassigned. |
| Storage/export | Private tenant scope, expiring retrieval, reauthorization, audit and deletion propagation. | Storage provider selection, tenant-selective export/restore, URL revocation and backup expiry evidence. | Data/storage owner unassigned. |
| Backup/restore | Isolated restore; preserve ledger/audit facts, tenant boundaries, retention and legal holds. | Scope, encryption, frequency, restore order, RPO/RTO, drill cadence and proof. | Operations/security owners unassigned. |
| Telemetry | Redact secrets and raw identifiers, restrict access, record source time/freshness. | Redaction rules, retention, cardinality, alert owners and incident runbooks. | Service owners unassigned. |
